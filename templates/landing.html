Platform

A Complete Record, From First Report to Final Filing

Every module here is built and running — not a mockup.

Offline AI Section Mapping

Keyword and fuzzy-match analysis reads the complaint narrative and suggests the correct IPC sections across 15 crime categories — no external API, no per-query cost.

FORM IF-1 Compliant FIRs

A 9-section form matching the official Indian police FIR format, with duplicate-number checks and instant, print-ready PDF export via ReportLab.

PIN-Gated Evidence Locker

Upload images, PDFs, and video linked directly to an FIR number, behind a PIN-protected access screen with sanitized, tamper-resistant storage.

Offline Law Library

Search and browse IPC, CrPC, and six other Indian acts from a bundled, fully offline reference database — no connectivity required to look up a section.

Citizen Complaints Pipeline

Citizens file a complaint, officers review and update its status, and an FIR number attaches automatically the moment it's filed — visible at every stage.

Case Outcome Predictor

Describe a situation and get a reference read on likely charges and sentencing range, informed by the same indexed legal data.

Enter the Narrative
